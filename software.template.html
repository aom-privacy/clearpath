What it does

One screen tells you if you’re winning or losing.

Control of the job after the budget: every line item, every payment, every change — and a forecast of what it will cost to finish.

Budget & payments

  1. Import or generate your budget — from Excel, OPUS or Neodata.
  2. Record payments by line item, with supplier, type and date.
  3. Subcontracts with overpayment flags.
  4. Only approved changes move the budget.

Forecast & control

  1. Cost centre: base budget, paid, committed and forecast, by line item.
  2. EVM/CPI forecast of the cost to complete.
  3. Traffic-light alerts when a line item trends over.
  4. S-curve and cash flow.

Reports & records

  1. KPI panel, PDF and Excel reports.
  2. Consistency check across the whole job.
  3. Change log with version comparison.
  4. Excel-style editing in every table.

Easy to learn, no cost specialist needed to run it, simple and economical — and still accurate and useful. Built by a cost controller, not a software company.
