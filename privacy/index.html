Privacy Policy — CLEARPATH App

Effective date: 24 November 2026.

1. What this policy covers

This policy applies to the CLEARPATH mobile app, the on-site companion to the CLEARPATH cost-control software. It does not cover the CLEARPATH website or the Windows desktop software.

2. No accounts, no personal data collected by the app

The app does not ask you to create an account or sign in, and it does not collect, store or transmit personal information about you to CLEARPATH. We do not receive your name, email address, location, device identifiers or usage data.

3. Your project data stays yours

Notes and project files you create in the app are stored on your device and, if you choose, in a cloud storage account you connect yourself (for example, Dropbox), so you can see your data on your computer. That connection is between you and your cloud provider: your data is stored under your own account and is governed by that provider’s privacy policy. CLEARPATH does not have access to your cloud account, and your project data is never sent to CLEARPATH servers.

4. Permissions

The app only accesses the files and folders you choose to open, save or share. When you attach a file or a photo, the app uses your device’s file picker, so it only sees what you select.

5. Third parties

The app contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools. If you connect a cloud storage service, that service’s own terms and privacy policy apply to the data stored there.

6. Children

The app is intended for adults using it for construction project work and is not directed at children under 13.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

8. Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at clearpathloscabos@gmail.com.
