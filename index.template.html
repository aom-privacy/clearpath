The problem

Building from abroad means trusting others with your money.

Small changes get approved on site and noted late — or never. The overrun is usually discovered at closeout, when there is nothing left to adjust.

Without weekly control

  • Small changes are approved on site and nobody records them in time.
  • At closeout, the build cost more than planned — and nobody knows exactly which line item it went to.

With weekly control

  • Every line item is reviewed while the work is in progress, not at the end.
  • If a line item is trending above its budget, it is adjusted in time — above all in labor.

A simple example

Take the masonry line item: $2,000,000 MXN set aside for labor, trending to $2,300,000. Seen in time, the crew is adjusted — idle time and extra hours avoided — instead of finding out at closeout.

On a $60,000,000 MXN build, losing this control can mean 5–10% extra — $3,000,000 to $6,000,000. One item caught in time can pay for months of control.
